DiffCode 0.4.5

修复

  • 终端里全屏程序的滚动。内置终端里，Codex、less、man 这类占满整个终端的程序，用鼠标滚轮或触控板滚动都没有反应。现在可以像在「终端」和 iTerm2 里一样滚动，需要用鼠标的程序也能收到滚轮。
  • 终端调高后的布局。把终端往上拖高、又没有可比较的内容时，侧边栏和变更列表上方会空出一块，终端最下面几行（编程助手等你输入的地方）也会跑到窗口外面。现在各列保持原位，diff 区域空着时放不下就少显示一些内容，终端最高只能拖到侧边栏还放得下的位置。窗口较窄时，diff 顶部那一栏会先隐藏统计和视图按钮，不再把终端挤歪。

Fixes

  • Scrolling in full-screen terminal programs. In the built-in terminal, the mouse wheel and the trackpad did nothing in programs that take over the whole terminal, such as Codex, less and man. They now scroll there as they do in Terminal and iTerm2, and a program that uses the mouse receives the wheel.
  • Layout with a tall terminal. With the terminal dragged up and nothing to compare, a gap opened above the sidebar and the Changes list, and the terminal's last lines, where a coding agent waits for your input, went below the window. The columns now stay in place, the empty diff shows less when it is short of room, and the terminal stops where the sidebar still fits. In a narrow window, the diff's header drops its counts and view buttons instead of pushing the terminal sideways.
